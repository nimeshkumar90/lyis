About This Course

The LYIS-Q is a qualification for those wanting to become an international school principal or those currently serving as one. It includes the fundamentally important roles of the International School Principal. The LYIS-Q is designed to be culturally and contextually robust, and practically relevant. Studied over 12-months, it is a sustainably focused school leadership qualification that is built around our mission and core values of Ethical Leadership, Opportunity and Sustainability.

"The LYIS-Q challenges how leaders are developed, empowering you to design inclusive recruitment practices, dismantle bias, and create schools where everyone can thrive, irrespective of their background."

Key Outcomes

Develop sound awareness of the role of the international school principal
Build your school culture and develop your people with integrity
Lead strategically and sustainably with impact
Navigate the right change at the right time with the right underlying purpose
Enhance the quality of your own professional reflection

Course Modules

1 The International School Principal
Explore the multifaceted role of leading an international school in today's complex educational landscape.
2 Developing People - Leading to Create Opportunities for All
Build strategies for inclusive leadership that develops talent and creates opportunities.
3 Curriculum and Learning Leadership
Lead curriculum design and implementation that meets international standards and local needs.
4 Sustainable Leadership and Governance
Develop governance frameworks that ensure long-term school sustainability and success.
5 Building Relationships to Navigate Change
Master the art of stakeholder relationships and leading through transformational change.
